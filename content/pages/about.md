## What we cover

District Times reports on local news, the District's eight wards and the neighborhoods within them, and politics and government in Washington, D.C. — the Mayor's office, the D.C. Council, Advisory Neighborhood Commissions and the federal decisions that affect District residents.

District Times is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any other newspaper, website or media organization with a similar name. If you are looking for a different publication, please check its web address.

## How we work

Every article on District Times is signed by its author, carries its real publication date and lists the sources it relies on, with links to primary records wherever they exist. When we cannot confirm something, we say so or leave it out. Read our full [editorial standards](/standards/) and our [corrections policy](/corrections/).
