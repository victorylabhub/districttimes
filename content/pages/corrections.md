---
title: Corrections policy
lede: When we get something wrong, we fix it and tell readers what changed.
updated: 2026-10-23
---
District Times corrects factual errors as quickly as we can confirm them.

- **Corrections** are added to the affected article in a clearly labeled note that states what was wrong and what is correct, along with the date of the change.
- **Updates** that add new information are marked with an "Updated" date at the top of the article.
- We do not remove published articles to hide errors. In rare cases where an article must be withdrawn (for example, for legal or safety reasons), we say so on the page.

## Report an error

Write to [info@atlasmedianetwork.org](mailto:info@atlasmedianetwork.org) with the article's address and the specific error. Please include a source if you have one.
